About CMMAI 2026

“The conference aims to foster a sense of intellectual community — grounded in mathematical rigour, driven by curiosity, and open to new perspectives.”

The Vision

The International Conference on Computational Mathematics, Modelling and Artificial Intelligence is a thoughtfully curated forum dedicated to the exchange of ideas at the intersection of mathematics, computation, and intelligent systems. It is envisioned as a space where rigorous mathematical theory meets modern computational practice, and where researchers gather to reflect on how foundational ideas are shaping contemporary science and technology.

The Impact

Computational mathematics has become a defining force in scientific discovery, enabling the understanding, prediction, and control of complex systems. From numerical analysis and scientific computing to stochastic modelling and optimization, mathematical thinking now plays a central role in addressing challenges in the physical, biological, economic, and data sciences.

The Dialogue

The conference aims to bring together a diverse yet closely connected community of researchers from academia, research institutions, and industry. It is designed to encourage meaningful conversations between established frameworks and emerging ideas, and between disciplines that may differ in language but share common mathematical foundations.

Future Horizons

By highlighting emerging directions including quantum computing, quantum-inspired algorithms, and their mathematical foundations, we create a space for reflection on future challenges. Above all, the conference fosters an intellectual community grounded in mathematical rigour, driven by curiosity, and open to new perspectives in shaping modern science.

About Bharata Mata College [AUTONOMOUS]

Bharata Mata College (Autonomous), established in 1965 by His Eminence the late Joseph Cardinal Parecattil, is a nationally accredited first-grade arts and science institution in the aided sector, affiliated to Mahatma Gandhi University, Kottayam. Managed by the Archdiocese of Ernakulam-Angamaly under the Bharata Mata Educational Trust, the college earned an A+ grade from NAAC in 2019 and is ranked by NIRF in the 100–150 band. Guided by its motto “For God and Country", the college upholds the ideals of love, service and dedication to the nation, while fostering holistic education, responsible citizenship, and inclusivity across gender, caste, and socio-economic backgrounds.

About Department of Mathematics

The Postgraduate and Research Department of Mathematics at Bharata Mata College, Thrikkakara, established in 1965, is distinguished for academic excellence and vibrant research culture. Offering undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes, the department has produced ten Ph.D. graduates and over 100 research publications in reputed journals. It regularly organises national and international conferences, seminars, and workshops, creating a dynamic platform for scholarly exchange and collaboration. Research thrust areas include Fluid Dynamics, Numerical Analysis, Applied Mathematics, Mathematical Modeling, Graph Theory, Topology, Fuzzy Algebra and Fuzzy Topology.
