Cultural & Natural Escapes

Discover God's Own Country

Enrich your academic journey by experiencing Kerala's unique charm, rich traditions, and unparalleled natural splendor. Make your visit to CMMAI 2026 unforgettable.

Alleppey

Alleppey Backwaters

65 km from venue
Famous for its serene backwaters, traditional houseboat cruises, lush village landscapes, and a rich tradition of coir and boat festivals.

Munnar

Munnar Hills

115 km from venue
A picturesque hill station nestled in the Western Ghats, known for endless tea plantations, mist-covered valleys, and cool weather.

Fort Kochi

Historic Fort Kochi

26 km from venue
Famous for its colonial architecture, Jewish heritage, Dutch Palace, St. Francis Church, Art cafes, and iconic Chinese fishing nets.

Athirappilly

Athirappilly Waterfalls

57 km from venue
Often called the "Niagara of South India", this is Kerala’s largest waterfall. Surrounded by lush forest, it's a breathtaking retreat.

More Hidden Gems

155 km away

Thekkady & Periyar

Rich in biodiversity with options for boating in the Periyar Lake. Home to wild elephants, bison, rare bird species, and vast aromatic spice plantations.

11 km away

Hill Palace Museum

Former royal residence of the Kochi Maharajas, showcasing Kerala's grand cultural legacy.

30 km away

Cherai Beach

A clean and quiet beach near Vypin Island, perfect for swimming and glorious sunset views.

Adjacent to Fort Kochi

Mattancherry

Home to the Paradesi Synagogue, Jew Town, and antique shops. A rich blend of Jewish, Dutch, and Malabar cultures.

55 km away

Kumarakom

A peaceful backwater village on Vembanad Lake, known for the Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary.

54 km away

Marari Beach

A serene, less-crowded destination known for eco-resorts and traditional fishing villages.

40 km away

Paniyeli Poru

A scenic riverside spot along the Periyar River, known for its natural rock formations and gushing rapids. Ideal for picnics.

95 km away

Vagamon

A lesser-known hill station with rolling meadows and pine forests. Ideal for paragliding.
