\begin{abstract}
\noindent

\noindent
People interested in submitting a contribution to CMMAI 2026 are invited to submit electronically an extended abstract no later than April 17th, 2026.

\medskip

\noindent
The abstract should clearly state the motivation of the study, the problem being addressed, and the methodology adopted. The key results and their significance should be briefly highlighted. Technical details, lengthy derivations, and figures should be avoided unless explicitly permitted.
\medskip

\noindent
The abstract must be self-contained and written in clear, concise language. Citations may be included if required. 

\medskip

\noindent
The abstract must be converted to Portable Document Format (PDF) before being submitted through the Conference website.

\medskip

\noindent
The extended abstract must be written in English using Times New Roman font. The body of the extended abstract should not exceed 1000 words.

\medskip

\noindent
Each abstract must include the full name and complete address of all author(s). In the case of joint authorship, the name of the author who will actually present the paper should be indicated with an asterisk ($*$).

\medskip

\noindent
Abstracts will only be accepted on the understanding that the work will be presented in person.

\end{abstract}
