About CMMAI 2026

The Vision

Welcome to the International Conference on Computational Mathematics, Modelling and Artificial Intelligence, a thoughtfully curated forum dedicated to the exchange of ideas at the intersection of mathematics, computation, and intelligent systems. The conference is envisioned as a space where rigorous mathematical theory meets modern computational practice, and where researchers gather to reflect on how foundational ideas are shaping contemporary science and technology.

The Impact

Computational mathematics has become a defining force in scientific discovery, enabling the understanding, prediction, and control of complex systems across an expanding range of disciplines. From numerical analysis and scientific computing to stochastic modelling, optimisation, and data-driven methodologies, mathematical thinking now plays a central role in addressing challenges in the physical, biological, economic, and data sciences, as well as in artificial intelligence.

The Dialogue

The conference aims to brings together a diverse yet closely connected community of researchers from academia, research institutions, and industry. It is designed to encourage meaningful conversations, between established frameworks and emerging ideas, between theory and computation, and between disciplines that may differ in language but share common mathematical foundations.

Future Horizons

With an emphasis on depth, dialogue, and intellectual openness, the conference offers a setting that values careful thought and genuine exchange. By highlighting emerging directions in computational mathematics, modelling, and artificial intelligence, including quantum computing, quantum-inspired algorithms, and their mathematical foundations, the conference seeks not only to present current advances, but also to create space for reflection on future challenges and opportunities.

Above all, the conference aims to foster a sense of intellectual community—one grounded in mathematical rigour, driven by curiosity, and open to new perspectives. It is an invitation to engage deeply, to share ideas freely, and to collectively explore the evolving role of computation and mathematics in shaping modern science.

About Bharata Mata College

Bharata Mata College is a premier institution of higher education located in Thrikkakara, Kochi, in the south Indian state of Kerala. The college is affiliated with Mahatma Gandhi University and is known for its academic excellence, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and dedication to research.

About The Department

The Department of Mathematics has a rich tradition of promoting analytical thinking and applied mathematical research. It hosts numerous academic events, workshops, and seminars aimed at fostering innovation and bringing students closer to global mathematical advancements.
