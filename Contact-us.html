Venue & Directions

Bharata Mata College is a premier institution of higher education located in Thrikkakara, Kochi, Kerala. Affiliated with Mahatma Gandhi University, the campus is equipped with modern academic facilities and is easily accessible from all major transit hubs.

By Air

The nearest airport is Cochin International Airport (COK), situated approximately 22 km from the college. App-based taxi services like Uber and Ola are readily available at the arrival gates for a direct commute to Thrikkakara.

Find Flights

By Train

Ernakulam Junction (South) and Ernakulam Town (North) are the nearest major railway stations, both located 10-12 km away. Pre-paid auto-rickshaws and cabs are available outside the stations 24/7.

Check Timings

By Bus

Thrikkakara is heavily connected by KSRTC and private bus networks from Vyttila Mobility Hub, Aluva, and Ernakulam city. Alight directly at the "Bharata Mata College Bus Stop" located at the main gate.

Bus Routes

By Car / Taxi

Situated on the prominent Seaport-Airport Road, the campus is easily navigable via GPS. Ample parking space is available within the campus for delegates and keynote speakers.
